Prince Berthram

KNOWLEDGE. DISCIPLINE. PURPOSE.

A lifetime of experience.
A mission to teach.

Prince Berthram Chidi Eke is a Justice of the Peace in Imo State, Nigeria. His biography spans over 30 years in business systems, leadership and quantitative analysis, alongside over 14 years of live trading in forex, indices and commodities.

Known to his audience as “Prof.”, he brings a disciplined, mechanical approach to market analysis, placing structured systems, trading psychology and risk control at the centre of education.

“Trading is not gambling; it is structured systems, psychology and rigorous risk control.”

EDUCATION & LEADERSHIP

Business leadership

Chairman and Managing Director of FSH Limited, Nigeria; Investor and Logistics Director at VATES Facilities Support WLL, Bahrain; Country Representative for Jollof House WLL Bahrain.
