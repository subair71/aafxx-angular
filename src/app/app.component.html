THE PERSON BEHIND THE METHOD

Prince Berthram
Chidi Eke (JP)

Founder, AAFXX · Author · Financial market educator

KNOWLEDGE. DISCIPLINE. PURPOSE.

A lifetime of experience.
A mission to teach.

Prince Berthram Chidi Eke is a Justice of the Peace in Imo State, Nigeria. His biography spans over 30 years in business systems, leadership and quantitative analysis, alongside over 14 years of live trading in forex, indices and commodities.

Known to his audience as “Prof.”, he brings a disciplined, mechanical approach to market analysis, placing structured systems, trading psychology and risk control at the centre of education.

“Trading is not gambling; it is structured systems, psychology and rigorous risk control.”

EDUCATION & LEADERSHIP

Grounded in systems.
Driven by learning.

Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence, according to his supplied biography.

Academic background

M.Sc. in Management Information Systems (Computer Science), Ambrose Alli University; Postgraduate Diploma in Computer Science, Federal University of Technology Owerri; NCE in Mathematics and Physics Education, University of Ibadan.

Business leadership

Chairman and Managing Director of FSH Limited, Nigeria; Investor and Logistics Director at VATES Facilities Support WLL, Bahrain; Country Representative for Jollof House WLL Bahrain.

THE AAFXX VISION

Help 1,000 people build
their financial knowledge.

The founder’s “1,000 Wealth Creators” mission connects practical books, MQL5 Expert Advisors, TradingView Pine Script indicators and wall-mount checklists, with and without coding. His planned four-book learning series begins with Book 1, Minor Daily, progressing from beginner to advanced study.

His work includes the AAFXX Triline System (EMA9, SMA20, MFI50, RSI50 and candle patterns at EMA9), five FINAL V3 Expert Advisors and BBB CMM trading systems.
